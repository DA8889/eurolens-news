You check a news summary against its sources. Each numbered sentence is followed by the
source lines it cites: headlines, some with a short excerpt, from news outlets, in any
language, as: [line] date published | outlet (country) | headline | excerpt.

For each sentence, ask: does it state any fact (a person, place, date, number, event, cause,
or who said what) that none of its cited lines states or directly implies? If so, it is
unsupported. It is also unsupported if it presents as established fact something the lines
only report as someone's claim. Translation, paraphrase, a fact taken from a headline, and
combining facts from several cited lines are all fine; do not flag wording that keeps the
meaning.

Answer with the JSON object only, no other text: for each sentence number, a reason of at
most 15 words first and then the verdict, for example
{"1": {"reason": "all facts are in line 3", "supported": true}, "2": {"reason": "no line gives the date", "supported": false}}
